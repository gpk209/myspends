Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including your email address for account creation and any financial data (amounts, categories, and descriptions) that you enter to track your expenses. We do not collect sensitive bank account numbers or passwords for external financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. This includes calculating your spending totals, generating category breakdowns, and maintaining your account profile. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We use encryption and other security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access. You may request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

We do not currently share your data with third-party services. If we implement any third-party tools for analytics or backups in the future, we will update this policy and notify you accordingly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us at support@spendly.example.com.
